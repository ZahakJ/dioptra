Compare / Excel

Dioptra vs Excel for lab data

Excel is the tool everyone has, and it is the first stop for most lab data. Dioptra replaces the part of that route where data get tidied, tested and plotted: text stays text, tests come with their assumption checks, and the graph is a journal figure. For everything else, Excel is the more capable spreadsheet.

02 In short

Which to use

Keep Excel for

  • sample sheets, inventories, plate maps and budgets;
  • work shared with people who do not install anything new;
  • files that many people edit at the same time.

Use Dioptra for

  • the step from instrument export to statistics and figure, where errors are easiest to miss;
  • tests that need post hoc comparisons, nonparametric alternatives, curve fits or survival curves;
  • figures sized for a journal, without rebuilding them in another program.

Dioptra opens .xlsx, .xls and .ods files directly, at the precision they were stored, and exports any table back to XLSX or CSV.
